# **Blockchain Enabled Transparent and Tamper-Proof Carbon Credit Trading Platform with Smart Contract Automation**

---

## 1. Introduction

Climate change has become one of the most critical global challenges. Industries release greenhouse gases such as Carbon Dioxide (CO₂), Methane (CH₄), and Nitrous Oxide (N₂O) during production processes. Governments therefore introduce **carbon credit systems** to control industrial emissions.

A carbon credit represents permission to emit **1 ton of CO₂ equivalent**.
Industries that emit less than the permitted limit earn credits, while industries exceeding the limit must purchase credits.

However, traditional carbon markets suffer from:

* Data manipulation
* Fake reporting
* Lack of transparency
* Delayed verification
* Trust issues between industries

To solve this, the proposed system uses **Blockchain Technology and Smart Contracts** to build a decentralized, transparent and tamper-proof carbon credit trading platform.

The platform automatically issues, transfers, and retires carbon credits using blockchain transactions, eliminating manual intervention and ensuring trust.

---

## 2. Objectives

The main objectives of the system are:

1. Monitor industrial emissions digitally
2. Automatically calculate carbon credits or penalties
3. Tokenize carbon credits on blockchain
4. Enable secure trading between industries
5. Prevent fraud and double-spending
6. Maintain transparent public ledger
7. Automate trading using smart contracts
8. Generate carbon offset certificates

---

## 3. System Overview

The system follows the **Cap-and-Trade Mechanism**:

1. Government (Admin) sets emission limits (baseline).
2. Industries submit yearly emission reports.
3. If emissions are lower than the baseline → credits are issued.
4. If emissions exceed the baseline → industry must buy credits.
5. Credits are traded on a blockchain marketplace.
6. Transactions are permanently stored on blockchain.
7. When credits are used, they are permanently retired.

---

## 4. User Roles

### 4.1 Admin (Regulatory Authority)

Represents the environmental regulatory body.

### 4.2 Industry

Represents companies/factories that emit greenhouse gases and participate in trading.

## 5. Blockchain Integration

The platform uses a blockchain network (Ethereum/Polygon Testnet) to ensure:

* Immutable records
* Transparent transactions
* Ownership verification
* Tamper-proof trading

### Blockchain is used for:

• Carbon credit token creation (Minting)
• Transfer of credits between industries
• Recording transaction hash
• Preventing double spending
• Credit retirement (offset emission)

Each carbon credit is represented as a **digital token**.

**1 Token = 1 ton CO₂ reduction**

Every transaction produces a **Transaction Hash**, which acts as proof of authenticity.

---

## 6. Smart Contract Automation

Smart contracts automatically execute actions without human intervention.

### Smart Contract Functions

1. **Mint Credits**

   * Triggered when emission is below baseline
   * Tokens issued to industry wallet

2. **Transfer Credits**

   * Triggered when buyer purchases credits
   * Tokens transferred to buyer wallet

3. **Retire Credits**

   * Triggered when industry offsets emissions
   * Tokens permanently removed (burned)

4. **Record Transactions**

   * All operations stored on blockchain ledger

---

### 7.2 Emission Reporting Module

Industry uploads emission data:

* Industry ID
* Sector
* Year
* Gas Type
* Reported Emission (tons)

An industry may submit multiple emission types.

---

### 7.3 Baseline Management (Admin)

Admin sets allowed emissions for each gas type and year.

---

### 7.4 Credit Calculation

#### Reduction Percentage

Reduction% = ((Baseline − Reported) / Baseline) × 100

#### Credits Earned

Credits = Baseline − Reported (if positive)

#### Penalty Credits Required

Penalty = Reported − Baseline (if positive)

---

### 7.5 Carbon Credit Tokenization

If emissions are below baseline:

* Smart contract automatically mints tokens
* Tokens stored in industry blockchain wallet

---

### 7.6 Marketplace Trading Module

Industries can:

**Sell Credits**

* List available credits
* Set quantity
* Sell at market price

**Buy Credits**

* View available listings
* Purchase credits
* Blockchain automatically transfers tokens

(No manual approval required)

---

### 7.7 Market Price Calculation

The carbon credit price is dynamic and depends on demand and supply.

**Supply (S)** = Total available unused credits
**Demand (D)** = Total penalty credits required by industries

Admin defines:

* Base Price (BP)
* Adjustment Factor (AF)

### Market Price Formula

Market Price = BP × (1 + AF × (D / S))

If demand increases → price increases
If supply increases → price decreases

---

### 7.8 Blockchain Ledger (Explorer Page)

### 7.9 Credit Retirement (Carbon Offset)

* Credits are burned via smart contract
